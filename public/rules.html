📋 Marketplace Rules

Junior eFootball Market

1. Be Respectful

Treat buyers, sellers and other members respectfully. Harassment and abusive behaviour are not allowed.

2. Honest Advertisements

Provide accurate information about every account or item you advertise. Do not mislead buyers.

3. Protect Your Information

Never share your M-Pesa PIN, OTP, email password or other sensitive login information.

4. Avoid Scams

Check information carefully before transactions. Report suspicious advertisements or users to the administrator.

5. Respect Account Ownership

Only advertise accounts or items you are authorized to offer. Never attempt unauthorized access to another person's account.

6. Follow Payment Procedures

Follow the marketplace's advertising-credit and payment procedures. Never submit false payment confirmations.

7. Report Problems

Contact the marketplace administrator if you encounter suspicious activity. Never send passwords or PINs when reporting a problem.
